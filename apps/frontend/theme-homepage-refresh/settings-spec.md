# Settings Spec

Mode: Work-led

## Structural Lock

- Keep section ordering.
- Keep provider controls in their current grouping.
- Keep feature flags and danger zone placement.
- Keep status modules near the top.

## Visual Intent

- Settings should feel operational, not playful.
- Status cards should be crisp and numerical.
- Controls should have strong readability and clear state.
- Danger styling should be refined, not alarming by default.

## Components To Reskin

- page shell
- status cards
- provider controls
- segmented options
- toggles
- destructive actions

## Tone

- precise
- trustworthy
- low-noise
