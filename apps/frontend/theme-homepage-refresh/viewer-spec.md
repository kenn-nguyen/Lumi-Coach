# Viewer Spec

Mode: Work-led

## Structural Lock

- Keep action cluster above the document.
- Keep title and feedback ordering.
- Keep resume paper placement and size relationship.
- Keep edit/download/delete flows untouched.

## Visual Intent

- Strong separation between chrome and paper.
- Feedback modules should feel curated and calm.
- The paper should remain the visual center of gravity.
- Shell treatment should stop before competing with the document.

## Components To Reskin

- top actions
- page title
- feedback cards
- document stage
- document surrounding controls

## Tone

- trustworthy
- document-led
- restrained
